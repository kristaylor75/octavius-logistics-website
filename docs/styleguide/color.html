§ 01 / COLOR OKLCH · CONSTANT L/C, VARYING H PALETTE
COLOR SYSTEM

Color & Palettes

A near-black instrument canvas, a cool neutral ink ramp, and five product signature hues — each derived in OKLCH so perceived lightness and chroma hold constant while only hue rotates.

§ 01.1 / NEUTRALS

Canvas & Ink

Surfaces ascend in lightness; ink descends. A faint blue-cool cast (H ≈ 255) unifies every neutral.

§ 01.2 / NEUTRAL RAMP

Full neutral scale

Nine steps at H 255 / low chroma — the structural greys behind surfaces, rules, and text.

§ 01.3 / SIGNATURE HUES

Per-product tonal ramps

Each product is one hue. Base sits at L 72 / C 0.16; the ramp extends to lighter tints (text/active states on dark) and deeper shades (subtle fills, contour bands). Chroma peaks near mid-lightness, as OKLCH gamut allows. The base step is marked ◆.

§ 01.4 / VARIANTS

Subtle · Base · Vivid

The three working tokens per product: subtle for fills/washes, base for data ink and accents, vivid for emphasis and active states.

§ 01.5 / USAGE

In context

DATA INK ON CANVAS
+12.4%

Signature hue carries data — readings, deltas, plotted lines. Never a background flood.

SUBTLE FILL + HAIRLINE

Subtle tone as a quiet panel wash, framed by a hairline in the base hue.

TEXT CONTRAST (WCAG AA)

ink · ink-muted · ink-faint label

ink & ink-muted pass AA for body. ink-faint is for ≥12px mono labels only.

SEMANTIC MAPPING

States borrow product hues rather than adding a new palette: success → green, caution → amber, alert → magenta. Keep semantic use sparing so product identity stays primary.

NOTE · GAMUT

Ramp values are authored in OKLCH and rendered by the browser. On displays without wide-gamut (P3) support, the most saturated steps clamp toward sRGB — they stay on-hue but read slightly less vivid. Verify final values against the deployment target before locking.
